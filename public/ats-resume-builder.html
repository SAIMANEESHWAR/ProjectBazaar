What is an ATS resume builder?

An ATS resume builder helps you create and optimize a resume so applicant tracking systems can parse sections and match keywords from a job posting. Students in India often need this for campus portals and company career sites.

How to improve your ATS score

  1. Open CodeXCareer resume tools.
  2. Paste the target job description and review missing keywords.
  3. Rewrite bullets with clear outcomes—without inventing experience.
  4. Export a clean, single-column layout recruiters and parsers can read.
  5. Build a portfolio and practice interviews before mass applying.
