What is an AI mock interview?

It is a practice interview run by software that asks questions, listens or reads answers, and scores communication and content so you can improve before human interviewers.

How it fits your prep loop

  1. Study topics in Preparation Mode.
  2. Drill coding questions and timed mock assessments.
  3. Run a live AI mock interview for delivery practice.
  4. Update your resume and portfolio with stronger stories.
