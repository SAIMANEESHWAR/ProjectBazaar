How AI mock interviews work

AI mock interviews simulate a real interview loop: an AI interviewer asks questions, enforces time pressure, and returns scored feedback so you can rehearse answers before campus or company rounds.

Key takeaways

  • Use AI for volume and structure; use humans for nuance.
  • Pair interview practice with coding assessments—not one or the other.
  • Rewrite resume bullets from stories that survived mock feedback.

Typical session flow

  1. Onboarding: role and difficulty
  2. Timed questions (behavioral and/or technical)
  3. Response capture
  4. Scored feedback and improvement notes

On CodeXCareer, start with the live mock interview demo, then use full sessions alongside mock assessments and coding interview questions.
