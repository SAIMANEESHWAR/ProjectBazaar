How to prepare for campus placements in 30 days

In 30 days, focus on high-leverage work: a parser-safe resume, daily coding and aptitude, one mock interview loop per week, and targeted applications—not random topic hopping.

Key takeaways

  • Week 1: resume + fundamentals. Week 2: DSA depth. Week 3: mocks. Week 4: apply.
  • Track weak topics; do not restart the curriculum every weekend.
  • Use one platform loop so practice feeds your resume stories.
