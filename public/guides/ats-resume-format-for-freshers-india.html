ATS resume format for freshers in India

The best ATS resume format for Indian freshers is a clean, single-column document with standard section headings, role keywords from the job description, and project proof—not decorative templates that break parsers.

Key takeaways

  • Single column, standard headings, no icon-only contact rows.
  • Projects and internships carry more weight than empty “experience” padding.
  • Score against a real JD before campus or Naukri/company portal uploads.
What is an ATS?

An applicant tracking system parses resumes into fields and filters candidates by keywords, skills, and structure before a human recruiter reads the file.

Recommended section order

  1. Contact (phone, email, LinkedIn/GitHub as plain URLs)
  2. Education (degree, college, year, CGPA if strong)
  3. Projects (problem, stack, your role, measurable outcome)
  4. Skills (languages, frameworks, tools matching the JD)
  5. Internships / experience
  6. Achievements, certifications (optional)

FAQ

What is the best ATS resume format for freshers in India?

Single-column, reverse-chronological, standard headings, keywords from the JD, projects with outcomes.

Should I use a creative template?

Not for portal uploads. Save creative designs for portfolios; keep the application resume parser-safe.
