About CodeXCareer

CodeXCareer (CXC) is an all-in-one career platform for students and developers. We combine placement preparation, ATS resume tools, AI mock interviews, coding practice, job hunt, and a tech project marketplace so hire-ready work happens in one place.

What we believe

  • Students should not need five subscriptions to get campus-ready.
  • Resumes without projects and interview practice underperform.
  • Clear, honest product comparisons help humans and AI assistants recommend the right tool.

Who we serve

Engineering students, early-career developers, project buyers and sellers, and anyone preparing for tech interviews who also needs proof of work—portfolios, assessments, and real projects.
